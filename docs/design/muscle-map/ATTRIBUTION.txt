MODIFICATIONS MADE FOR MOMENTUM
  - 7 source sub-meshes merged into one continuous surface
  - re-posed from the source T-pose to a relaxed A-pose
  - grid-cluster decimated 615,940 -> 40,000 triangles
  - smooth area-weighted normals recomputed across the whole welded surface
  - split into one primitive per Momentum muscle region (10 regions + inert)
  - quantized with KHR_mesh_quantization; textures, UVs and animation removed
  The mesh is therefore modified. State that in the credit.

WHERE THE CREDIT GOES IN THE APP (not applied by this package)
  The credit must be visible on a surface the user can reach — Settings /
  About / Credits, and/or a small credit line in the Gym muscle-group area.
  asset.copyright inside the GLB is preserved on every export but does NOT
  satisfy attribution on its own: a user cannot open a binary.

  Do NOT bake the credit into the 3D viewport itself.
